This folder stores ONLY June3 and June4 compressed archives (1,191,175,553 bytes). Provider digests/sizes are in the v2 FREEZE.json and release_metadata.json. Prior June5-7 archives are preserved in their original folder. Counting all five selected session partitions does not recover full population demand. Other-date sessions can have missing calls in this window. Call counts include unsuccessful attempts and are not token demand, GPU/VM requirements, or SLO outcomes.
